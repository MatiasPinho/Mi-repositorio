# Visual review rubric

## Hierarchy
Can a student infer the document structure in 15–20 seconds? H1, H2, H3, prose and supporting material must have unmistakable roles.

## Readability
Body type, measure, leading and paragraph spacing support sustained reading. Flag lines that feel too long/short, cramped or excessively airy.

## Density
The page should primarily look like calm, carefully edited university notes in the **Carpeta notebook system**. Flag card soup, repeated boxes, oversized chrome, excessive labels, strong shadows, loud sidebars and empty space that disconnects related concepts. The notebook grammar may be visible, but it must not compete with learning.

## Signaling
Semantic color/rails must guide attention selectively and remain visually quiet. Meaning must survive grayscale/color-vision differences through labels and structure. Full tinted boxes should be rare.

## Figures
Instructional figures are large enough to inspect, visually close to their explanation and captioned with what to notice. Derived figures should read as deterministic notebook/pencil sketches at their real display size. A technically valid SVG that becomes visually clean, tiny, crowded or ambiguous after embedding is a failure.

For Visual System V2, inspect both wide and narrow scene evidence. Reject clipping, unreadable text, crowding, elements that look accidentally stuck together, ambiguous arrows, routes through text, excessive density, weak hierarchy or a mobile composition that depends on zoom. A model-generated composition is not allowed to approve itself; its preview requires a separate vision-capable review bound to the actual PNG hashes.

## Notebook grammar
Course/type/scope/title should form a coherent notebook/chapter opening rather than an app toolbar. Ruled paper, the restrained binding margin/holes, selective marker emphasis and handwriting/pencil accents are intentional product grammar. Tables and code remain precise learning objects rather than decorative sketches.

## Artifact-specific chrome
A summary or rapid review should read as a document without persistent sidebar navigation. A long guide may use a quiet editorial TOC and thin reading-progress line, but both must remain secondary to the paper.

## Editorial measure
Prose measure and figure/table width are different concerns. Ordinary paragraphs should remain around a comfortable reading line length even when technical figures or tables use more of the page width.

## Responsive
Mobile must preserve reading order and avoid horizontal prose scrolling. V2 derived scenes use a separately designed `narrow` geometry with the same semantic elements rather than shrinking an oversized wide composition.

## Print
No clipped blocks, broken page flow, dark theme leakage or unnecessary navigation. Major headings should not strand at the bottom of a page. Pencil figures must remain legible in print.

## Accessibility
Check contrast, focus visibility, semantic labels and non-color meaning. Alt/caption content remains required for figures.

## Anti-slop checks
Fail or strongly penalize:
- gradients used only to make the page feel modern;
- unrelated stickers, scrapbook decoration, heavy fake grain, distressed paper or vintage cosplay;
- strong drop shadows or floating-card styling;
- uniform rounded cards around every section;
- pill badges everywhere;
- huge hero taking a screen before useful content;
- decorative numbering that does not represent a real sequence;
- ornamental flourishes or drop caps without a learning function;
- excessive motion;
- visual novelty that competes with studying.

Do **not** penalize the canonical ruled notebook paper, binding cues or deterministic pencil sketches merely for being notebook-like; those are the active product direction.
