# Figures

A figure earns space when it reduces the amount of mental reconstruction the learner must do.

Prioritize figures for:
- spatial architecture;
- process/state transitions;
- hierarchies;
- timing/concurrency;
- topology/relationships;
- before/after transformations;
- complex comparisons.

Place a figure immediately after the sentence or paragraph that establishes why it matters. The caption tells the learner what to notice, not merely the filename or source.

Do not use decorative images. Do not shrink dense source diagrams until labels are unreadable. Prefer full-width treatment for complex technical diagrams.
