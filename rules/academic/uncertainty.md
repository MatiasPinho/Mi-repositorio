# Uncertainty contract

Use four assessment-relevance states:
- `confirmed`: explicitly supported by sufficiently authoritative evidence.
- `likely`: strong evidence or course structure suggests it, but it is not confirmed.
- `unknown`: not established.
- `excluded`: explicitly established as out of scope.

Unknown stays unknown. Do not infer that a final is cumulative, that a unit belongs to a partial, or that a professor's tentative comment is a formal rule.

Teaching emphasis and assessment scope are separate signals. Repetition, “important”, “ojo”, student confusion or extended explanation can increase study priority, but do not turn content into confirmed exam scope.
