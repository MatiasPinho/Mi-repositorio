# Assessments and Argentine university semantics

Keep distinct: parcial, recuperatorio, final, integrador/coloquio, TP, promoción, regularidad and correlativas.

For each concrete assessment resolve, when supported:
- id/name and type;
- date;
- format;
- parent relationship (for example which parcial a recuperatorio recovers);
- confirmed/likely/unknown/excluded scope;
- grade/eligibility rules;
- source and confidence.

Never fill missing grade thresholds, promotion conditions, regularity duration, final opportunities or correlations from convention.
