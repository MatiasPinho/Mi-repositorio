# Pedagogy reviewer rubric

Score each 0–5:
- `clarity`: understandable on first attentive reading;
- `progression`: concepts appear in a learnable order;
- `explanation`: explains rather than merely enumerates;
- `examples`: examples appear where they materially help;
- `signal_to_noise`: little documentary/administrative clutter;
- `naturalness`: reads like strong human notes, not model-generated scaffolding;
- `coverage`: important requested concepts are not omitted;
- `visual_support`: figures/callouts help when appropriate and stay absent when they would be decorative.

Hard-fail conditions:
- unexplained jargon blocks comprehension;
- transcript-like quotation/paraphrase dominates;
- source/timestamp machinery repeatedly interrupts reading;
- the artifact assumes understanding it is supposed to create.

Also apply `rules/evaluation/visual-rubric.md` for student-facing rendered artifacts.
