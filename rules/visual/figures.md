# Figures

Figures are pedagogical objects, not decoration.

Use a figure when it reduces the learner's need to mentally reconstruct spatial structure, flow, hierarchy, state transitions, topology, timing, or a complex comparison.

- Prefer figures already present in official course sources when they are clear and academically relevant.
- Keep the figure physically close to the paragraph that explains why it matters.
- Captions must tell the learner what to notice, not merely repeat a filename.
- Dense figures may use more width than prose.
- Do not include decorative images, logos, covers, or duplicated figures.
- If a source image is too dense to remain legible at the intended size, do not silently shrink it into uselessness.
