# Study document

Student-facing material is a long-form learning document, not an audit report, dashboard, landing page, or collection of cards.

The default visual thesis is a **modern academic book**:
- warm paper-like surface without fake texture;
- dark high-contrast ink;
- restrained semantic color;
- one dominant reading column;
- calm chapter hierarchy;
- figures and tables treated like textbook material;
- almost no interface chrome.

Semantic differences should remain visible but quiet. Definitions, examples, warnings, errors, connections, and recall blocks may have distinct roles without becoming a rainbow of components.

Summaries and rapid reviews should read as centered book pages with no persistent sidebar. Long guides may have a quiet editorial table of contents when navigation genuinely helps.
