# Rapid review artifact

Designed for material already learned and a real 5–10 minute pass.

Include only high-yield cues: central model, essential definitions/distinctions, formulas/conditions, common traps and retrieval prompts. Prefer a small diagram over a paragraph when reconstruction of that diagram is itself useful recall. No course administration unless the requested review is specifically for an assessment. No long explanations or source archaeology.

The rapid review is rendered to the same HTML study surface, but with denser spacing and more retrieval emphasis than a normal summary.
