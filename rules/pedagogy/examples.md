# Examples

Use examples to remove ambiguity, not to decorate text.

A good example:
- is small enough to understand immediately;
- demonstrates exactly one or two relevant ideas;
- appears near the concept it clarifies;
- does not introduce unnecessary new terminology;
- matches course conventions when that matters.

Do not paste full BASIC/Prolog/C++/Java programs merely because they appeared in class. Show only the fragment needed for the learning objective unless the full program itself is under study.
