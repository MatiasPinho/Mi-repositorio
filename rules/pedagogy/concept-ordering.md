# Concept ordering

Order by dependency and comprehension, not by file order.

Before writing:
- identify the unit's central question or purpose;
- identify prerequisites;
- cluster concepts into a small number of coherent sections;
- introduce terminology only after the reader has enough context to understand it;
- delay exceptions/conflicts until the base concept is clear;
- move administrative course information out of conceptual notes unless it directly changes how to study the requested scope.

Prefer a narrative progression over a taxonomy dump.
